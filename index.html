BeatingU: Vision Challenge

Live on the App Store

Test your vision.
Beat your friends.

A 60-second visual sprint you compete on — not a wellness quiz. Find the gap in 12 shrinking rings. Accuracy wins; speed breaks the tie.

  • ◯ The 20/20 Sprint. Spot the opening in each Landolt C ring and tap its direction before the next one shrinks.
  • ▲ Up to 1,500 points. Twelve rounds, one score — built to be screenshotted, not just read.
  • ★ Rivals, not reminders. Scores post to Game Center, and you can challenge a friend straight from your result.

BeatingU: Running Challenge

Coming soon

Hit GO.
Just run.

Pick a distance — half a mile up to ten. Pocket your phone. GPS stops the clock the moment you cross the finish, and your time goes straight to the leaderboard.

  • ⏱ Six races, six leaderboards. 0.5, 1, 2, 3, 5 and 10 miles — each one with its own ranking.
  • ▶ No stopwatch to fumble. Live splits, pace and distance remaining — and timing keeps going with your phone locked.
  • ★ Beat your best, then theirs. A live “vs your best” readout, split-by-split results, and Game Center challenges.
